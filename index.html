ButterflyBlades

Two sisters. Ten trials. A thousand stolen wings.

A hack & slash for Sofija & Emilija

How to Play

  • Move — WASD / arrows, or the left thumbstick.
  • Attack — J / Space, or the ⚔ button. Tap fast to chain combos.
  • Special — K, or the ✦ button. Costs Flutter energy.
  • Dodge — L / Shift, or the » button. Brief invulnerability.
  • Rescue — enemies carry caged butterflies. Free every one to open the gate.
  • Swap — press Q or the sister portrait to tag in the other sister mid-fight.
